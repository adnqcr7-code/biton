Sound

Engineered to get out of the way.

Every part of ONYX One exists to remove something: noise, weight, distortion, waiting. What's left is the recording, exactly as it was mixed.

01 — DRIVER

A driver with one job

A custom 40 mm dynamic driver with a beryllium-coated diaphragm. Tight, controlled bass that never bleeds into the mids — and highs that stay clean at full volume.

02 — ANC

Silence that adapts

Six microphones sample the world 48,000 times a second and cancel it before it reaches you. Step outside and the ANC re-tunes itself for wind, traffic and crowds.

03 — SPACE

Room sound, anywhere

Head-tracked spatial audio places instruments in a room around you instead of inside your skull. Turn your head — the stage stays where you left it.

Design

Built like something you'll keep.

No visible screws. No creaks. A frame that folds flat and a finish that doesn't show fingerprints — because the best object in your bag is the one you stop noticing.

FAQ

Asked, answered.

Can I use them wired?

Yes. The 3.5 mm cable in the box works with the headphones powered on or off — powered off, they behave as passive headphones.

How does the 30-day return work?

Use them for a month. If they aren't for you, send them back for a full refund — no restocking fee, no questions beyond "what didn't work?".

Are the cushions replaceable?

Yes. They twist off in seconds, and replacements are sold on this site. The headband cushion is replaceable too.
